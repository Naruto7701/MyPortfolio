REEL / 03 — ABOUT

I started editing on a laptop in a college dorm room, cutting skate videos down to the frame that mattered. Seven years later the footage has changed — commercials, documentaries, narrative shorts — but the job hasn't: find the cut that makes the audience feel something before they know why.

I work closely with directors and DPs from dailies through final delivery, and I'm comfortable owning color, sound design, and finishing when a project needs a single hand across the whole post pipeline.

Based in Los Angeles. Available for remote and on-site projects worldwide.
